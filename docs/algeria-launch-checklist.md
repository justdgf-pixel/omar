# Algeria launch checklist for digital products

This document is a practical starting point for launching an Algeria-focused
store for ebooks, design packs, online courses, templates, and other digital
goods.

## 1. Business and compliance basics

- Confirm the correct legal structure for your activity.
- Make sure your registration covers e-commerce and digital sales.
- Validate invoice requirements, VAT treatment, and record retention with a
  local accountant or legal adviser.
- Keep clear order logs for every delivered digital product.

## 2. Payments to plan for

Your production checkout should usually prioritize:

- **CIB** for bank card users
- **Edahabia** for broad reach through Algerie Poste
- **Support fallback channels** such as WhatsApp for payment guidance and
  customer reassurance

Potential gateway paths often mentioned in the local market include providers
that support CIB and Edahabia integrations. Before selecting one, verify:

- merchant onboarding requirements
- payout timing
- webhook support
- sandbox/testing quality
- settlement and transaction fees

## 3. Product strategy that works well

The easiest digital products to launch first are usually:

- ebooks and guides
- social media or business templates
- course bundles
- private communities and memberships
- software licenses or downloadable assets

Start with products that have:

- clear before/after value
- instant delivery
- low support overhead
- pricing simple enough to explain in one screen

## 4. Website MVP checklist

- Landing page with a clear promise
- Product catalog with pricing in DZD
- Product detail pages with screenshots and outcomes
- FAQ about payment, delivery, and refunds
- Checkout with trusted local payment messaging
- Delivery confirmation page with clear next step

## 5. Mobile app MVP checklist

- Browse featured products
- Save favorites or recent views
- Simple checkout handoff
- Order history and redownload access
- Push notifications for delivery and promotions

## 6. Backend features to build next

- Authentication
- Product management dashboard
- Orders table
- Payment status tracking
- Webhook ingestion
- Download tokens or course enrollment automation
- Analytics for conversion and repeat purchases

## 7. Marketing channels to test

- Instagram and TikTok short-form content
- Creator partnerships
- WhatsApp community or broadcast lists
- Telegram or email for delivery and upsells
- Niche audience landing pages by product type

## 8. Operational rules to define early

- Refund policy for digital goods
- Delivery timing promises
- Support response windows
- Abuse prevention for repeated downloads
- Terms of use and licensing language

## 9. Good immediate next move

Once the brand and catalog are clear, build the backend around:

1. products
2. orders
3. customers
4. payments
5. downloads

That keeps the architecture small while still being enough for a real launch.
